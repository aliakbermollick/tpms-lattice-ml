TPMS Young's modulus calculator

Predicts the effective Young's modulus of a TPMS lattice with the Stacked Ensemble surrogate model.

Loading Python and the model (first load can take 20–40 seconds)…

The model was trained on finite-element results for 270 designs (five families, unit cell sizes 2–4 mm, wall thickness 0.30–0.40 mm, solid modulus 1.5 GPa, cylinder 12 mm × 20 mm). Predictions outside these ranges are extrapolations and are not reliable. The result is the as-designed stiffness; printed parts can differ because of fabrication effects. Everything runs in your browser, and no inputs are sent anywhere. Code and data: github.com/aliakbermollick/tpms-lattice-ml.
